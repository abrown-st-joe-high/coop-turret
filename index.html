CO-OP TURRET

Player 1W A S D steer · hold Q to fire
Player 2Arrow keys steer · hold Enter to fire
BothSpace = big blast (recharges) · P pause

One shared crosshair. Full speed needs both players pushing the same direction. One player alone, or different directions, only crawls. Opposite presses cancel.

Holding one fire key = 1x rate. Both fire keys together = 4x. Cruisers need the big blast.

Dogfight Run: a small ship chases enemy fighters and cruisers, shooting from behind.
